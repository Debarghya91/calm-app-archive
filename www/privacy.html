Privacy notice.

Draft for product review · 17 November 2026

Before public release, the publisher must add its legal name, registered address, privacy contact, retention periods, hosting details and applicable jurisdiction-specific disclosures. This draft is not a substitute for legal review.

1. Who operates SoulSpace?

SoulSpace is currently a development product. The legal entity responsible for this service and its privacy contact have not yet been supplied. Do not publish this page until those details are completed.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, SoulSpace may process your email address, display name, mood check-ins, journal entries, sleep diary entries, saved activities, activity progress, companion-language preference and activity feedback, as well as companion messages. You should avoid entering information you would not want processed by an online service.

3. Where information is stored

  • On-device: preferences (including companion language), activity feedback, mood history, journal entries, sleep diary and any conversation history you choose to keep are stored in browser/device storage.
  • Account data: when signed in, mood check-ins, journal entries, saved activities, progress and sleep diary entries are sent to the SoulSpace backend for account sync.
  • Conversation memory: companion messages are saved to your account only when you enable Conversation Memory. Turning it off deletes saved chat history from the account. Without an account, enabled conversation memory remains on that device.
  • Authentication: passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes. The current client stores the session bearer token in local browser storage.

4. AI companion processing

When you send a message while the live AI service is configured, the message, selected companion language and recent conversation context needed to answer are sent from the SoulSpace server to Anthropic's API. The current server does not persist the selected language as an account profile field. The AI provider processes that request under its own terms and privacy documentation. Do not use SoulSpace for emergencies or as a substitute for clinical care.

5. Why information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, maintain your account, synchronise your data, operate the companion, enforce service limits, protect safety and improve reliability. This build does not implement advertising profiles or sale of mental-health data.

6. Your controls

You can turn conversation memory off, export account data, delete your account and its associated cloud records, or delete locally stored app data from Profile. Account deletion does not automatically erase separate copies you may have exported or data retained by an external AI provider under its policies.

7. Security and retention

The current build uses password hashing, bearer sessions and account-level deletion. It has not yet undergone an independent security audit, penetration test, clinical review or production privacy assessment. Encryption at rest, formal retention schedules, backup deletion procedures and a complete incident-response process must be confirmed before launch.

8. Children and sensitive use

The service is not designed or validated for unsupervised use by children. Do not rely on the app for diagnosis, medication decisions, treatment or emergency response.
