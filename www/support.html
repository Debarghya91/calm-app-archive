Using the app

Try refreshing the app and checking your internet connection. The self-guided library, journal and ambient sound generator can work without the live AI service, subject to your device and browser.

Account and privacy

Use Profile to export your data, turn conversation memory off, or delete your account and associated cloud data. You can also delete device data from Profile.

Urgent support in India

For an emergency, call 112. For mental-health support, call Tele-MANAS 14416. If possible, stay with someone you trust. SoulSpace is not an emergency service.
